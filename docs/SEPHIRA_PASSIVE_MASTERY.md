# Sephira passive mastery allocation

This is the current MR allocation. The scope correction swaps eight command/LB-bound traits with existing general awakening bonuses, without adding or removing a passive or changing AP budgets. Christine’s True Stagger is an awakening bonus; MR7 grants Fill LB on Stagger. The same rule applies to Barusa, Minfilia, Trance Terra and Nalu.

All 131 distinct passives from the 15 excluded original visions remain in the complete kits. Command/LB-bound traits stay with their vision; MR rewards transfer between visions. The other 19 slots retain distinctive equipment bonuses and Knight of the Sacred Frost. Each vision has exactly one passive at MR 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9. Active MR skills return to awakening; all active recipes, permanent stat budgets and Spirit rewards are retained. Passives are native individual effects, without combined effects or extra grants at a rank.

Some passives move between visions to make room for the complete original collection. Lenneth keeps Physical Counter with Lustrous Shield; Fryevia keeps her spellblade trio and gains Knight of the Sacred Frost. Redundant permanent imbues are omitted where the kit already earns the same elemental imbue through attacks.
