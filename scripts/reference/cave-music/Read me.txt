Cave crystal and native movie music diagnostics

1. Run Install cave and music trace.cmd. Select your full game folder.
2. Restart Resonance. Enter the cave behind Rain's house and press F8.
3. Use Crystal Fina resonance once; press F8 during or just after the movie.
4. Run Collect cave and music trace.cmd and attach the returned ZIP.

No Studio build/install or Prepare Game is needed. The observer only reads
state and writes logs; it does not load levels, play audio or change gameplay.
It uses your existing Studio UE4SS loader and its owned observer folder.
Run Disable cave and music trace.cmd and restart to remove the observer.
